Neurogastroenterology · Emory University

How diet and environment wear down the second brain.

> currently decoding: enteric ferroptosis

I study the enteric nervous system, the neuronal network that runs the gut. My work asks how Western diet, iron-dependent ferroptosis and nanoplastic exposure damage enteric neurons and their mitochondria, and how that damage drives motility disorders and neurogenic inflammation.

Arun Balasubramaniam, PhD · Postdoctoral Fellow, Division of Digestive Diseases Emory University School of Medicine · Dr. Shanthi Srinivasan Lab NIH/NIDDK K99/R00 Principal Investigator
